# Legal and safety boundaries

This project is scoped to interoperability, preservation research, and privately operated sessions using lawfully acquired PC copies.

- Keep the repository private during discovery.
- Do not distribute copyrighted game binaries or extracted proprietary assets.
- Do not bypass ownership, entitlement, account, privilege, ban, or anti-cheat controls.
- Do not connect modified clients or compatibility services to official matchmaking.
- Use controlled accounts and machines; do not capture unrelated users' traffic.
- Do not retain or publish authentication secrets.
- Use EAC-disabled launch modes for modded-client research where supported.
- Synthetic local identity must be clearly distinct from Microsoft/Xbox-issued identity.
- Any public release needs a separate legal and security review.
